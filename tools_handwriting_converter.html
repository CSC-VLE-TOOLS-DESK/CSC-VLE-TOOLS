About Handwriting to Word, PDF & Text

Convert a photo or scan of a handwritten page into editable text, then save it as a Word file, a PDF or a Notepad text file. It reads English and Hindi. Reading happens in your browser, so your pages are not uploaded to our servers.

  • Free to use, with no sign-up or login
  • Reads many pages one after another, without freezing the screen
  • Neat, clear handwriting on plain paper works best. Always read and correct the text before saving.

Please check the final result before you submit or print it. See our disclaimer.
