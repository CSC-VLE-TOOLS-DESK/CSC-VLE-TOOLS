Hindi district names are available for Haryana only. Other states show English district names.

Maps are simplified for study and general reference. District boundaries follow Census 2011 with changes up to about 2016, so newer districts are not shown. They are not legal boundary records. For an official map, use Survey of India.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose World, India, or State / UT and pick any state or union territory from the list. Choose English or Hindi.
  2. Choose labels: none, names, or numbers with a list. Blank outline with numbers is good for a quiz.
  3. Optionally highlight one state or district, and add a title or a Name / Class / Date line.
  4. Click Print for an A4 sheet (choose Save as PDF in the print window if you want a PDF), or download a PNG or SVG file.
