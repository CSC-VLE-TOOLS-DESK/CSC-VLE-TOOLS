Background Remover – transparent PNG
Colours being removed: –
How to use: (1) Upload. The top and upper sides of the photo are treated as background and the centre as the subject. (2) If something is wrongly removed (for example a white shirt on a white wall), choose Mark SUBJECT and paint a few strokes inside it, then it re-calculates. (3) If background is left, paint it with Mark BACKGROUND. (4) Use Hair detail 6–20 for hair edges. Tap a colour chip to remove it from the list.

About Background Remover Pro

Remove a photo background with hair detail, brush touch-up and white, blue or any colour fill. Background Remover Pro is a free online tool on CSC VLE TOOLS, made for CSC and VLE centres, cyber cafes, students and job seekers.

  • Free to use, with no sign-up or login
  • Runs in your browser, so your files are not uploaded to our servers
  • Works on mobile, tablet and desktop
