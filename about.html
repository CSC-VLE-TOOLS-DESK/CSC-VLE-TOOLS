About CSC VLE TOOLS

Free, private and practical tools for the people who run the digital service counters of India.

What is CSC VLE TOOLS?

CSC VLE TOOLS is a free collection of web tools made for Common Service Centre (CSC) and Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE) operators, cyber cafes, photo studios, students and job seekers. It brings the small jobs that take up most of a counter day into one place: resizing photos and signatures to an exact size in KB, printing ID cards at actual size, joining and splitting PDFs, making resumes and application letters, billing, and quick calculators.

Who is it for?

  • CSC and VLE centre operators who handle online forms every day
  • Cyber cafes and photo studios that print ID cards and passport photo sheets
  • Students and job seekers filling exam and recruitment forms
  • Small shops that need simple billing and a daily ledger

What makes it different?

  • Free and simple: no sign-up, no subscription.
  • Private: tools run in your browser, so your photos and documents are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Made for Indian forms: Aadhaar, PAN, Haryana resume and application formats, English and Hindi support.
  • Print-ready: actual-size layouts and A4 sheets that are ready to print.

What you will find

Photo and image tools, ID card and print layouts, PDF tools, government application and affidavit formats, resume and document makers, receipts and billing, calculators, exam tools and quick utilities, plus Pro Studio and Shop Ledger. Browse them all on the home page.
