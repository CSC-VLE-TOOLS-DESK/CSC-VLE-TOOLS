About Image Merger

Join several images into one picture, vertical or horizontal, with spacing and background colour. Image Merger is a free online tool on CSC VLE TOOLS, made for CSC and VLE centres, cyber cafes, students and job seekers.

  • Free to use, with no sign-up or login
  • Runs in your browser, so your files are not uploaded to our servers
  • Works on mobile, tablet and desktop

Please check the final result against the latest official instructions before you submit or print it. See our disclaimer.
