About Web Search

Use this page to search with Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, YouTube, Wikipedia or Google Maps, or to open a website by typing its address. It is a handy start page for CSC and VLE centres and cyber cafes.

  • Free, with no sign-up or login
  • Your search words are sent only to the search engine you choose, and we do not store them
  • Results always open in a new tab
