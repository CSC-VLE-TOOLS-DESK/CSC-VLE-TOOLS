About Question Paper Maker

Create a printable question paper with MCQ and descriptive questions. Question Paper Maker is a free online tool on CSC VLE TOOLS, made for CSC and VLE centres, cyber cafes, students and job seekers.

  • Runs in your browser, so your files are not uploaded to our servers
  • Works on mobile, tablet and desktop
